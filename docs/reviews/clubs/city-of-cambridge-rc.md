## What's good

- **Achievement-led homepage**: Provincial Pennant WEHoRR 2026, 26 athletes at Henley, head of the river since 2023/2025, GB junior selections — countable, current, credible
- **LTR course fully specified**: 8 dated sessions, £99 all-in, what happens after (novice squad), plus a casual alternative via the Cambridge Rowing Experience partner
- **LTR booking via British Rowing justgo** — no form backend needed, payments handled by the governing body's system
- Instagram feed embedded — homepage stays fresh without manual updates
- "Estd. 1863" heritage woven into the brand
- Persistent JOIN NOW button in the header

## What's poor

- "View our squads" and other homepage card links point back to the homepage — same broken pattern as Lea
- Heading hierarchy chaos (h6 in header, h4 hero, h5 course sections)
- Skip-to-content implemented as a button, not a link
- Fees not visible on the pages reviewed
- JOIN NOW is a dropdown, not a direct link — one extra step

## Worth adapting

- **F-22: Book LTR courses via British Rowing's justgo/ClubHub events system** — payments and bookings handled by the NGB, no custom backend needed (feeds R-14)
- **F-23: Embed the club's Instagram feed** on the homepage for freshness without editorial effort
- **F-24: Partner-route for casual LTR demand** — signpost commercial taster providers so the club course stays focused on joiners
