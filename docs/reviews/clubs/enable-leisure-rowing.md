## What's good

- **Everything is bookable online** — junior courses, holiday camps, adult courses via Bookwhen; memberships via Gymflow. The only reviewed site with a complete e-commerce journey
- **Complete product ladder**: taster → junior courses by school year → holiday camps → adult beginner/intermediate → private coaching → improver sessions → recreational membership
- **"No membership commitments"** positioning — lowers the barrier for beginners
- School-partnership products (yearly/termly/one-off taster) — B2B revenue stream
- Specific, honest product copy (stable "tubs" up to fine racing boats; sculling focus with sweep possible)
- Charity + company numbers in footer; newsletter capture

## What's poor

- "Find out more" link goes to a raw Squarespace staging URL (`bluebird-carrot-kkzr.squarespace.com`) — migration not finished
- No photography on the rowing page itself
- Newsletter modal interrupts first visit
- Duplicate gtag config (3 console errors)

## Worth adapting

- **F-25: Third-party booking systems (Bookwhen/Gymflow)** — off-the-shelf course booking with payments, no custom backend; strong candidate for PCRC LTR courses (feeds R-14)
- **F-26: Product ladder from taster to membership** — structure rowing products as a progressive ladder with a clear next step at every level
- **F-27: School-partnership offer** — market termly/yearly school rowing packages alongside public courses (relevant to PCRC's lake and junior programme)
