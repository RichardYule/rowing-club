## What's good

- **Four clear homepage paths**: About, Join, Learn to Row, Corporate Events — each with image + CTA
- **Dedicated members portal** (members.learc.org.uk) for joining, event sign-ups and member directory — proper separation of public site vs member tools
- **Regattas on their own site** (learegattas.org) and **weddings/venue on their own brand** (theboathouseonthelea.co.uk) — commercial arms cleanly separated
- Charity number + company number in footer — trust signals
- **Photographer credits** in footer — good practice, respects copyright
- Recruitment posts with salary bands in news — professional
- "Support Us" in main nav

## What's poor

- **Card text links point to the homepage**, not their target pages — only the button links work; confusing for screen readers and users
- Safeguarding and Resources pages live on Wix default URLs (`copy-of-main-content-template`) — sloppy
- Fees not visible on the public site (behind members portal)
- Wix performance overhead; dated visual style
- Heading hierarchy jumps (h1 → h4 → h5 in footer)

## Worth adapting

- **F-16: Separate members portal** for joining/event sign-up — keeps the public site simple while giving members real tools
- **F-17: Photographer credits** in footer — cheap goodwill with the people who shoot club photos
- **F-18: Job/volunteer recruitment posts with salary bands** — signals a professional, funded organisation
