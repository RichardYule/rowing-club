## What's good

- **Three audience cards on homepage**: Learn to Row, Row with Leeds (experienced), Cox our Crews — the only site reviewed that actively recruits coxes with their own path
- **LTR page structured as a numbered journey** (1. ergo intro, 2. water sessions, 3. free novice sessions, 4. choose your pathway) — sets expectations better than any other club
- **Waiting-list honesty**: "you may have to wait 6 months or more" since the 2024 Olympics — with an embedded form to capture the lead anyway
- Junior academy intake rules stated plainly (Year 8 testing, no late entry after Year 8 usually)
- Facilities story: £1m boathouse + Waterloo Lake park venue — two named venues with pages
- Members Area in main nav

## What's poor

- No events or results section in the main nav
- Homepage title tag is just "Leeds RC" — weak SEO
- Some pages text-only without imagery
- Fees not visible from the pages reviewed (buried or absent)

## Worth adapting

- **F-12: Audience-split "Get Involved" cards** — separate cards/paths for beginners, experienced rowers, and coxes
- **F-13: Numbered LTR course structure** — show the course as steps (what happens week by week) to demystify it
- **F-14: Waiting-list capture form with honest wait times** — convert demand even when courses are full
- **F-15: Dedicated cox recruitment path** — coxes are scarce; market to them explicitly
