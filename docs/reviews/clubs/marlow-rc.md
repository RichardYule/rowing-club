## What's good

- **Six programme cards** covering every audience — including **Adaptive (Para-Rowing Centre of Excellence)** and **Recreational**, plus Gym & Go-Row memberships that monetise the gym without rowing
- **Four LTR products** including a **free learn-to-cox course** run by experienced coxes — solves the cox shortage directly
- **Traffic-light river status banner** at the very top of every page ("wear sunscreen beware the heat") — safety/status messaging with zero clicks
- **Venue hire as a first-class section** (weddings, celebrations, meetings, public bar "Sunset-on-Thames") integrated on the club domain rather than a subsite
- Two hosted regattas with their own nav section
- Alumni/Parents nav section — keeps leavers and families engaged
- Donate in main nav; charity + trading-company numbers in footer; photographers credits page
- Junior squad marketed with countable evidence (HWR/HRR record, alumni in GB squads/Olympics)

## What's poor

- Heading hierarchy chaotic (h5 labels, multiple h1s per page)
- Duplicated homepage title tag
- Slider-heavy homepage; news not prominent
- "We're Making A Move" recruitment card links to a document URL, not a styled page

## Worth adapting

- **F-30: Adaptive/para-rowing programme visibility** — a named inclusive programme signals welcome beyond token accessibility statements
- **F-31: Free learn-to-cox course** — removes the pay barrier for the scarcest role; pairs with R-09's cox recruitment path
- **F-32: Status banner at top of every page** — traffic-light water/safety status with zero clicks (feeds R-11)
- **F-33: Alumni/parents section** — a lightweight engagement home for leavers' families and past members
