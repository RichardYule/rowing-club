## What's good

- **Pricing cards** showing monthly and annual side by side — scannable fee presentation
- **Honest capacity messaging**: "the 2026 courses are full, keep your eyes out for L2R 2027" — manages demand without losing the lead
- Hero leads with a current, countable achievement (Victor Ludorum 2026)
- Membership types explained in plain language (who qualifies for what, peak/off-peak rules)
- **Venue hire separated into a dedicated site** — keeps the rowing site focused while monetising facilities
- Map + address + phone repeated in footer of every page

## What's poor

- Joining journey ends at a mailto — no form anywhere
- Broken YouTube embed on the homepage ("This video is unavailable")
- Decorative characters and unlabelled icon links — accessibility issues
- Dated theme with slider; heavier page weight
- No junior-specific content despite running junior programmes
- Instagram link points to a hashtag explore page, not the club account

## Worth adapting

- **F-05: Pricing-card presentation** of fees (monthly alongside annual) — more scannable than a wide table on mobile
- **F-06: Honest capacity messaging** for courses ("2026 full — watch for 2027") keeps demand warm
- **F-07: Venue/facility hire as separate subsite** — relevant to PCRC's lake hire offering
