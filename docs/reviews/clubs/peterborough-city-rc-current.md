## What's good

- Full fee table with pro-rata columns, family discounts, rack fees, EA licence — genuinely complete fee information
- Named committee contacts with direct mailto links (Membership Secretary, Club Secretary)
- Hardship fund documented — rare and welcoming
- Real photography (full club photo hero, squad photos)
- 128 news posts — an active press function exists
- Four hosted events a year clearly listed

## What's poor

- Joining journey split across three sections (Join form under Squads, fees under About Us, LTR sign-up separate)
- Empty landing pages (Home, About Us, Squads, Events, Contact) — content lives in sidebars/widgets
- Broken "Read More" link on homepage (empty href)
- No H1 on the homepage; no `lang` attribute; heading hierarchy jumps straight to h4
- Dated visual design; wide fee table scrolls on mobile
- Results page empty; course records duplicated across two pages
- "The Big Build" (completed appeal) still top-level nav

## Worth adapting

- Nothing structural — this is the site being replaced. Its fee-table completeness and named-contact pattern are already carried into the new build.
