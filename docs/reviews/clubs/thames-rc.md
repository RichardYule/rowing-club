## What's good

- **Honest positioning**: states plainly it has no juniors and no learn-to-row — saves everyone time and shows confidence
- **Events commercialised properly**: thamesrcevents.com with dedicated socials — a real revenue arm, not a page
- **Donate in main nav** — fundraising made prominent (charity-status club)
- Separate **history archive subsite** for heritage content
- Company registration number + registered address in footer — trust signals
- Visiting rowers page — serves a real London-river need
- Gap-year rowing FAQ — niche funnel done well

## What's poor

- **Fees as an inline text blob** ("· Full: £730· Under-25: £520· …") — worst fee presentation of any site reviewed
- Stray `/new-page` link left in body copy
- Homepage nearly empty below hero
- Joining form is an external Microsoft Form — leaves the site, no brand continuity
- "click here" link text

## Worth adapting

- **F-08: Dedicated events subsite/socials** for hosted regattas (aspirational; PCRC's four events could grow this way)
- **F-09: Donate/fundraising in main nav** once charity fundraising pages exist
- **F-10: Visiting-crew page** — PCRC hosts visiting crews for lake hire; a "visiting rowers" page (parking, boathouse, course map) would serve them
- **F-11: Honest scope statements** — say clearly what the club does *not* offer to prevent wasted enquiries
