## What's good

- **Squad sections sell with evidence**: "11 GB vests won in 2025", "Victor Ludorum past 2 British Championships", Henley wins — credibility, not adjectives
- Membership table shows **monthly direct debit alongside annual** price, and **strikes through categories that are full** — honest capacity signalling
- **Indoor learn-to-row course** (£40/4 weeks) as a low-commitment funnel into summer water courses — clever product design
- River status & safety given a main-nav slot
- Fresh, regular news with results written up as stories
- Detailed membership notes (who can use what, open-club inclusion statement, data protection)

## What's poor

- **Three domains in active use**: nav links to `wallingfordrowingwebsite-kiegx.wordpress.com`, policies link to `wallingfordrowing.co.uk`, site itself on `wallingfordrc.co.uk` — confusing and SEO-diluting
- Footer "Team" and "History" links are dead (`#`); "Subscribe" link has empty href
- No enquiry form anywhere — every contact is a mailto
- No hero photography; text-led homepage
- Heading hierarchy broken (h3 before h1)

## Worth adapting

- **F-01: Achievement-led squad marketing** — lead squad sections with countable results, not adjectives
- **F-02: Capacity signalling on fees** — strike-through or badge for membership categories that are full
- **F-03: Indoor learn-to-row course** as a funnel product with clear dates/price
- **F-04: River/water status page in main nav** (PCRC equivalent: lake status)
