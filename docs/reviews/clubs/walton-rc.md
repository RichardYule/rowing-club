## What's good

- **Live river-conditions embeds**: Thames Conditions Live widget (flow, stream warnings, water temp) + GaugeMap river-level gauge with a plain-English threshold ("if over 3.45m it is over the landing-stage") — automated, always-current safety info
- **FAQ-style join page** answering what people actually ask: cost (£150–200 course + subscription), age ranges (12 to 90), commitment levels, safety (50m swim standard)
- Audience-split joining paths for four personas
- Honest demand signalling: junior courses "always oversubscribed — plan several months ahead"
- Fresh, categorised news feed with results as stories
- Hosted head race (Walton Small Boats Head) in main nav

## What's poor

- Dated theme; news feed as homepage with no hero or joining CTA above the fold
- Contact is an obfuscated email address — no form
- Three third-party iframes on every page hurt performance
- No h1 on inner pages; unlabelled social links
- Site served over http, not https
- Members Area is a stale forum

## Worth adapting

- **F-28: Live water-conditions embed** (Thames Conditions Live / GaugeMap) — automated status with a plain-English threshold; direct input to R-11 (lake status) without manual updates
- **F-29: FAQ-style join page** — answer cost / age range / commitment / safety questions inline where people actually look
