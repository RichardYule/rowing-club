The 2026 Peterborough Summer Regatta delivered everything we hoped for: fast
side-by-side racing on the 1000 m and 500 m courses, visiting clubs from across
the country, and a bank full of spectators making the most of the sunshine.

Highlights from the weekend:

- Strong entries across junior, senior and masters events on both days
- Course records under pressure in the afternoon tailwind
- The BBQ, clubhouse catering and Pimms stall running flat out from first
  race to last

Full results are published on the external results systems linked from our
[events page](/events/#results).

## Thank you, volunteers

A regatta simply doesn't happen without the small army of volunteers who run
it — from stakeboat operators and start marshals to the catering, bar and
Pimms teams. Thank you to everyone who gave their weekend.

Fancy helping next time? Have a look at the
[volunteering roles](/support/#volunteering) — no experience needed for most
of them.
