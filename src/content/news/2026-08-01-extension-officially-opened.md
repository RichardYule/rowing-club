The club's new extension was officially opened on **Saturday 1 August 2026**,
marking the end of a fundraising effort that began life as "The Big Build" back
in 2018.

The finished project is a little smaller than the original vision, but no less
valuable: the old conservatory has been replaced by a **large multi-purpose
room**, and the **kitchen has been extended** to cope with regatta days and
club socials. The new room is already hosting erg sessions, squad briefings
and Wednesday club nights.

## Thank you

This simply wouldn't have happened without members and friends of the club:

- everyone who **bought a brick**, organised events or donated along the way;
- the volunteers who painted, shifted, cleaned and fitted out the new space;
- and sponsors like **[Bridewell](https://bridewell.com)**, whose team spent a
  day stripping the old railings ready for painting.

You can read more about the project — and how to support the club's next
goals — on the [Support the Club](/support/#the-extension) page.
